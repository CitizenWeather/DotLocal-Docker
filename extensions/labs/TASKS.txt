# TODO: State, not just services
Most "lab" platforms are stateless — you spin them up, knock them down. localnet could persist world state:

- Snapshot the entire intranet (DNS records, registrations, certs, users, data) and restore
- Branch a localnet like a git branch — fork the world, experiment, merge or discard
- Time-travel debugging at the infrastructure level

This is the killer feature nobody else has. Docker has snapshots of containers; nobody has snapshots of internets.
